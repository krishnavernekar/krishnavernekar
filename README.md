# Featured Projects

## 01. TripRescue

### Agentic AI-Powered Travel Disruption Recovery Platform

<p align="center">
  <img src="assets/triprescue.png" width="100%" alt="TripRescue project preview"/>
</p>

TripRescue is an agentic AI-powered travel disruption recovery platform designed for high-stakes journeys. It goes beyond conventional travel chatbots, flight search tools, and booking aggregators by acting as an autonomous decision and recovery agent governed by authoritative deterministic constraint engines.

When an upstream disruption occurs, such as a flight delay or cancellation, TripRescue analyzes downstream consequences and helps identify feasible recovery options across multimodal travel networks.

**Core Capabilities**

- **Cascade Failure Prediction:** Predicts downstream disruptions across connected travel itineraries.
- **Multimodal Recovery Planning:** Generates alternative recovery plans involving flights, trains, buses, and emergency transit hotels.
- **Transfer Viability Verification:** Evaluates whether physical transfers between transport connections are feasible.
- **Insurance & Compensation Analysis:** Evaluates potential insurance coverage and compensation eligibility.
- **Verified External Handoff:** Prepares verified external booking handoffs for user review and approval.

**Engineering Focus:** Agentic AI · Decision Systems · Deterministic Constraint Engines · Multimodal Planning · Travel Recovery Workflows

**Repository:** [github.com/krishnavernekar/triprescue](https://github.com/krishnavernekar/triprescue)
